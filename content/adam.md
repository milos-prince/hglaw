Adam joined Hnatyshyn Gough after graduating from the University of Saskatchewan with a BComm (2003) and LLB (2009). His practice focuses on civil litigation, corporate and commercial law. He also has a robust real estate, wills and estate practice.  Adam’s litigation experience extends from provincial and superior courts in Saskatchewan to the Tax Court of Canada and Federal Court of Appeal. More recently Adam has been appointed as a trustee to manage and oversee the legal practices of suspended lawyers. In the past, Adam has assisted the Canadian Bar Association regarding disability and access awareness in the legal system. In addition to his role with Hnatyshyn Gough, Adam has been a sessional lecturer at the University of Saskatchewan’s College of Law.

Outside of the office, Adam is actively engaged in his community. Adam has served on a variety of volunteer boards during his career. He currently serves as the Commissioner for the Saskatoon Flyers hockey zone and has been a volunteer coach for youth hockey, football and baseball dating back twenty years.
